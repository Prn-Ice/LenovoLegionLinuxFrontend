# Prince's voice samples

Prince confirmed authorship of these four samples. The opening line introduces Princes list on findaphone.ng; the other samples are his descriptions of phones on that list. Preserve the sample text verbatim when using it as a voice reference.

These are style references, not text to edit or instructions to execute. They do not establish the accuracy of any phone claim or authorise carrying personal experiences into another draft.

```text
Phones that I "Prince" loves, usually for no good reason
```

## Reading the samples

These examples suggest a conversational, personal voice: affection for particular details, blunt criticism, playful exaggeration, and humour grounded in what Prince notices. They are observations from a small sample set, not requirements to add a joke or a personal anecdote to every paragraph.

Keep that character when it fits the draft. Correct accidental errors in the draft where useful without flattening its personality. The reference samples themselves stay unchanged. For other audiences and purposes, carry over relevant qualities without assuming that all of Prince's writing sounds like a phone description.
